Apply kaisey karein?

  1. Job card kholo aur poori detail parho — qualification, age limit aur last date check karo.
  2. Apply sirf official tareeqe se karo — jo link ya address likha hai, usi par.
  3. Documents pehle se tayyar rakho: CNIC, domicile, taleemi asnaad, CV aur photos.
  4. Kisi ko fee mat do. Government jobs ke liye koi paisa nahi lagta.

⚠️ Fake jobs se bacho

  • Agar koi WhatsApp/SMS par "fee bhej do, naukri pakki" kahe — 100% fraud hai.
  • Official ad ke baghair kisi link par CNIC ya bank details mat bhejo.
  • Shak ho to yahan ya Facebook page par pooch lo — jawab mil jayega.
