Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Pinterest application "islamic home decor pins" (the "App") handles information. The App is a private, single-user tool owned and operated by Hamza Zahid. It is not a public service, it has no sign-up, and it is used by exactly one person: the account owner himself.

Who operates the App

The App is operated by Hamza Zahid, a solo content creator who runs the Pinterest accounts @instamodelai ("Smart Finds Daily") and @hamzacivil09. Questions about this policy can be sent to hamzacivil09@gmail.com.

What the App does

The App allows its owner to batch-schedule and publish his own Pins — images, titles, descriptions, and links that he prepares himself — to his own Pinterest boards using the official Pinterest API (v5). It exists solely to replace repetitive manual posting in the Pinterest app and website.

What data the App accesses

Through Pinterest's standard OAuth authorization flow, and only with the owner's own authorization, the App accesses:

  • Pinterest account basics: the owner's username and account ID, used to confirm the App is acting on the correct account.
  • Boards list: the names and IDs of the owner's own boards, so Pins can be published to the correct board.
  • Pin content supplied by the owner: the images, titles, descriptions, destination links, and tags he prepares for publication, plus the Pins the App itself creates (so their publication status can be confirmed).

The App does not access data belonging to any other Pinterest user. It does not collect data from visitors, followers, or any third party.

How the data is used

Data is used for one purpose only: publishing the owner's own Pins to his own boards on the schedule he sets (approximately 10 Pins per day). The data is never used for advertising profiling, never analyzed for any other purpose, and never combined with data from other sources.

No sale or sharing of data

  • Personal data is never sold.
  • Personal data is never shared with third parties.
  • The only external system the App communicates with is Pinterest's official API, which is the service being operated.

Access tokens and storage

  • The App authenticates using OAuth access tokens issued by Pinterest.
  • Access tokens are stored securely and are used only to make the authorized API requests described above.
  • Tokens are never written into published content, never shared, and never exposed to any other person.
  • The owner can revoke the App's access at any time from his Pinterest account's connected-apps settings; revocation immediately stops the App from accessing the account.

Data retention and deletion

  • Pin content the owner prepares is retained only as long as needed to publish it and confirm publication.
  • No database of personal information about any third party is created or kept.
  • The owner may request deletion of any data associated with the App at any time by writing to hamzacivil09@gmail.com; such requests will be completed promptly, and in any case within 30 days.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored credentials, including restricted access to the system that runs the App. No method of storage is completely infallible, but access to the App and its tokens is limited to the owner alone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
